Turbojet Engine in Fusion 360

A 9 part axial flow turbojet engine, modeled in Fusion.
For my 3rd CAD it was a nightmare but atleast I understand how jet engines works, and by applying the "draw it yourself" helped me understand it way better.
Overall size: 400 mm long, 126mm max diameter, 19 separeted bodies.


The trubojet can:

Pull air in ussing the spinning compressors.
"Condence" the air by the difference stages of blade.
Fuel sprays, oxidiser sparys, combustion chamber and ignition thinggies.
Hot gas rushes out the back where it also drives the compressors at the front
(The combination of the spinning turbine in the shaft where the compressor is also located can help the engine to feed itself once its running.) 


Honest limitations

This is a geometry study, not an engineering design. Things it does not do:
No aerodynamic analysis. Blade angles were chosen to look and behave plausibly, not calculated from velocity triangles
No stress or thermal analysis
No bearings, seals, mounts, fasteners, or accessory gearbox
No inlet cone or nozzle guide vanes ahead of the turbine
Tip clearances are a flat 1 mm, not derived from thermal growth
Do not attempt to build this as a working engine. Real turbomachinery involves spinning parts at enormous speeds and continuous combustion, both of which are genuinely dangerous without proper engineering, materials, and testing
